Problem Statement
Organizations and universities find it difficult to identify what skills their employees or students lack and what they need to learn to achieve their career goals.
Traditional learning platforms provide the same courses to everyone without considering their existing skills, learning progress, or career interests.
Your Challenge
Build an AI-powered learning platform that understands an individual's skills, identifies missing skills, and recommends a personalized learning path to help them achieve their target career.
Expected Features
- User Profile: Maintain learner details, existing skills, certifications, projects and career goals.
- Skill Analysis: Identify strengths and weaknesses based on skills and assessments.
- Career Matching: Compare current skills with the requirements of a selected job role.
- Learning Recommendations: Suggest relevant courses, tutorials and practical exercises.
- Progress Tracking: Monitor learning progress and assessment performance.
- AI Mentor: Provide guidance and update learning recommendations based on individual performance.
Agentic AI Requirement
Use multiple specialized AI agents that collaborate to analyze skills, recommend learning resources, evaluate progress and guide learners.
The system should be capable of adjusting its recommendations when a learner changes their career goal or performs poorly in an assessment.
Sample Scenario
A student wants to become a Full Stack Developer.
- Current skills: Python, SQL and basic HTML.
- Missing skills: JavaScript, React, Node.js.
- Target: Become a Full Stack Developer.
Your platform should identify the skill gaps, create a learning roadmap, recommend resources and track the student's progress.
Expected Deliverable
A working AI-powered application that demonstrates personalized learning recommendations, AI agent collaboration and adaptive progress tracking.
